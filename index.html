Study Tips for Students

1. Pomodoro Technique

Study 25 minutes, take 5 minute break. Repeat 4 times. Helps with focus.

2. Active Recall

Don't just read notes. Close your book and try to remember key points.

3. Practice Problems Daily

For Math and Physics, solve 5 problems daily. Theory alone is not enough.
