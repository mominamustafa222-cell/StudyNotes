Chapter 1: Vectors

1.1 Scalar & Vector

Scalar: Magnitude only - Mass, Time, Temperature

Vector: Magnitude + Direction - Force, Velocity, Acceleration

Addition: R = √(A² + B² + 2ABcosθ)
Dot Product: A·B = ABcosθ
Cross Product: A×B = ABsinθ n̂

Chapter 2: Kinematics

2.1 Equations of Motion

1st: v = u + at
2nd: s = ut + ½at²
3rd: v² = u² + 2as
Where u=initial velocity, v=final, a=acceleration, s=distance, t=time
Example: A car starts from rest, accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5s. Find final velocity.
Solution: v = u + at = 0 + 2×5 = 10 m/s

2.2 Projectile Motion

Range: R = (u²sin2θ)/g
Max Height: H = (u²sin²θ)/2g
Time of Flight: T = (2usinθ)/g

Chapter 3: Newton's Laws

3.1 Three Laws

1st Law: Law of Inertia - F=0 then a=0

2nd Law: F = ma - Force = mass × acceleration

3rd Law: Every action has equal opposite reaction

Example: Force of 10N acts on 2kg mass. Find acceleration.
Solution: a = F/m = 10/2 = 5 m/s²

3.2 Friction

Static Friction: fs ≤ μsN
Kinetic Friction: fk = μkN
Where μ = coefficient of friction, N = Normal reaction

Chapter 4: Work, Energy & Power

4.1 Work

Work: W = F·s·cosθ
Unit: Joule (J) = N·m
Work is zero if θ = 90°

4.2 Energy

Kinetic Energy: KE = ½mv²

Potential Energy: PE = mgh

Work-Energy Theorem: W = ΔKE = ½m(v² - u²)

4.3 Power

Power: P = W/t = F·v
Unit: Watt (W) = J/s

Chapter 5: Circular Motion

5.1 Angular Variables

Angular Velocity: ω = θ/t = 2π/T

Angular Acceleration: α = ω/t

Relation: v = rω, a = rα

5.2 Centripetal Force

Centripetal Force: Fc = mv²/r = mrω²
Centripetal Acceleration: ac = v²/r = rω²
